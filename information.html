Christmas Light Show Info

The light show has 1345 lights that's 4035 channels.

To program 1 song three minute long it takes about 6-7 hours.

We have spent more than 50 hours perfecting the show.

The light show takes 39.6 kilowatt hours every year that's 142,560 Kilojoules! For reference a 60 Watt light bulb uses 60 joules every second!

When is the show running?

The music light show runs every half hour first show starts at 15:30 (3:30pm) last show starts at 20:00 (8:00pm)

Each light show lasts about 20 minutes.

The lights will be up from the 1st of dec to 2nd of Jan.
